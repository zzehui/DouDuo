A small studio for small tools.

DOUDUO builds small tools, and intends to stay for a long time: finish one tool properly, then build the next. Slower is fine — what we ship has to hold up.

Vision

Technology changes the world. Craft changes daily life.

Changing the world is an outcome, not a slogan. Whether technology reaches daily life comes down to specifics: does it work offline, does it leave you alone, and is there someone to fix it when it breaks. We get those right and let time do the rest.

What we care about

  • One step less. If it can be one step, don't make it three.
  • Say what breaks. Unsupported formats and known limits go in the manual, not in your bug report.
  • Don't intrude. No upsell popups, no auto-renewal, no treating your files as data.
